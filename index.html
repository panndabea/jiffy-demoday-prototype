Problem

Most presentations fail to keep people engaged.

Attention drops within minutes.

Only a small part of the audience actively participates.

Presenters rely on separate tools, manual prompts or awkward pauses to bring people back in.

Sharing links or slides quickly becomes a GDPR headache.

Solution

Make interaction part of how you present.

Participants join in seconds via link, QR code or session code.

No app download.

No login.

Smartphones become interaction devices.

Engagement happens during the presentation.
